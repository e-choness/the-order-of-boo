## 🎃 Every Halloween, a child makes a secret Midnight Wish.

The **Order of B.O.O.** — a secret society of small, very polite bedsheet ghosts — drifts down chimneys
to find out what it *really* is, and whether the child has earned a **Treat** 🍬 from Old Jack o' the
Lantern… or a **Trick** 🕷️ from the Bogeyman.

**Sneak** past parents in costume, cats who can *see* ghosts, dogs who can *smell* them and motion-sensor
lawn skeletons who **shriek** the moment you move.

**Snoop** through diaries, receipts, phone screens and sleep-talk — and watch out for decoys.

**Decide.** Get the wish and the ledger right to earn your promotion to Grand Phantom.

### 👻 Your spells
- **Fade** — turn see-through (cats aren't fooled)
- **Possess** — hop inside a jack-o'-lantern
- **Spirit Sight** — see through a skull's eyes
- **Bump in the Night** — a knocking will-o'-wisp lure
- **Snuff** — blow out a candle from across the room
- **Slumber Mist** — send someone to dreamland

### 🏚️ Four hauntings
Ghost School · The Hollowell House · Thornbury Farmhouse · Ravensworth Manor
